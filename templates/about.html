About NICT

25 years of shaping innovation, research, and industry leaders in computing.

Our Story

Founded in 2001, the National Institute of Computer Technology began with 60 students and a single vision: to make world-class computing education accessible to every deserving learner.

Today, we are a NAAC A+ accredited institution serving over 5,000 students across undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs. Our alumni lead product teams at Google, Microsoft, Amazon, and hundreds of pioneering startups worldwide.

Our 40-acre smart campus houses advanced research labs, a 24/7 digital library, startup incubation space, and a dedicated career development centre — everything required to transform curious minds into industry-ready professionals.
