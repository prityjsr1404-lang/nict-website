Our Journey

Founded in 2001, the National Institute of Computer Technology (NICT) began with a single vision: to make world-class computing education accessible to every deserving learner in Jamshedpur.

Today, we are proud to be one of the leading computer training centers, with two sprawling branches in Bagbera and Laxminagar.

Our alumni are working in top MNCs, and our school students consistently secure perfect 100/100 scores in ICSE, ISC, CBSE, and JAC boards. We believe in practical learning, which is why we maintain a strict 1-Student 1-PC policy in our labs.
